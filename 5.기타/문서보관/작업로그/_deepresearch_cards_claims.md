## C1 (vote 0-0)
The optimal learning strategy is not universal but depends on the kind of knowledge (stable facts vs. flexible procedures) AND the target learning process (schema induction vs. memory/fluency building) — directly supporting the research question's premise that application/procedural cards and fact-recall cards should be treated differently rather than via one uniform card type.

## C2 (vote 0-0)
For learning a novel procedure on an IMMEDIATE test (when long-term retention is not crucial), repeated studying of worked examples was MORE optimal than repeated testing/retrieval practice — i.e., retrieval practice is not strictly superior for procedural/application learning; worked examples can beat testing depending on retention interval.
출처: https://andymatuschak.org/files/papers/Yeo,%20Fazio%20-%202019%20-%20The%20optimal%20learning%20strategy%20depends%20on%20learning%20goals%20and%20processes2.pdf

## C3 (vote 0-0)
For long-term retention of a novel procedure (1-week delayed test), repeated testing was AS effective as repeated studying with nonidentical (varied) problems that enhance schema induction, but MORE effective than repeated studying with identical problems — meaning retrieval practice and worked examples are both viable for flexible procedures but operate via different mechanisms, and variation across practice items matters.
출처: https://andymatuschak.org/files/papers/Yeo,%20Fazio%20-%202019%20-%20The%20optimal%20learning%20strategy%20depends%20on%20learning%20goals%20and%20processes2.pdf

## C4 (vote 0-0)
Repeated retrieval practice improved not only retention (recall of definitions/facts) but also APPLICATION/transfer to novel situations, outperforming both restudy and quiz-study conditions on application final-test questions. This is directly relevant to the research question of whether separate 'application/포섭' cards are needed: it suggests retrieval of conceptual content can itself carry over to application, though only under specific conditions (see other claims).
출처: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475225001434

## C5 (vote 0-0)
The transfer/application benefit of retrieval practice is CONDITIONAL on amount of retrieval and delay: a single retrieval attempt with a short (8-minute) delay produced no benefit, whereas three retrieval quizzes with a one-week delay produced higher scores on both recall AND application questions. This bears directly on daily-drill/SRS design — application benefits emerge from spaced, repeated retrieval, not one-shot quizzing.
출처: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475225001434

## C6 (vote 0-0)
Retrieval enhances the RECOGNITION component of transfer — i.e., it helps overcome the 'inert knowledge problem' where learners possess the relevant knowledge but fail to apply it because they do not recognize a situation as one where it applies. This is a mechanism directly applicable to legal 쟁점 spotting (recognizing which rule/요건 applies to a fact pattern).
출처: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475225001434

## C7 (vote 0-0)
Flashcard (Anki) benefits are concentrated in foundational, fact-based knowledge assessed by standardized exams, and are weaker/more variable for higher-order clinical reasoning and course-based exams — the single study of Step 2 CK (more application-oriented) found no significant benefit (252.5 vs 247.0, p=0.440).
출처: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40670-026-02643-5

## C8 (vote 0-0)
Anki's default scheduler is based on SuperMemo's SM-2 algorithm, and a newer algorithm (FSRS, Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) has been introduced to improve interval optimization based on user performance — but the review presents this descriptively and provides no comparative outcome evidence that FSRS improves exam performance over SM-2.
출처: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40670-026-02643-5

## C9 (vote 0-0)
The evidence base is observational and low-strength: 11 studies (2015-2025, n=1,135 students), none randomized, predominantly cross-sectional/cohort, so reported associations cannot establish that Anki causes higher scores rather than diligent students self-selecting into Anki use.
출처: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40670-026-02643-5

## C10 (vote 0-0)
In multi-step mathematics tasks, learning with worked examples reduced cognitive load and imposed less subjective challenge than unguided problem solving on each individual solution step, with cognitive load diminishing as steps advanced toward task resolution.
출처: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01443410.2023.2273762

## C11 (vote 0-0)
Worked examples produced benefits on a transfer test to conceptually different problems, not only on similar/retention tests — specifically, transfer of reasoning knowledge was found when worked examples were given.
출처: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01443410.2023.2273762

## C12 (vote 0-0)
When the goal was to remember the verbatim text of a worked example (stable-fact-like recall), repeated testing was MORE effective than repeated studying after a 1-week delay — confirming the classic testing-effect advantage holds specifically for fact/verbatim retention, the regime where retrieval-practice (e.g., rule-name, cloze, holding) cards apply.
출처: https://andymatuschak.org/files/papers/Yeo,%20Fazio%20-%202019%20-%20The%20optimal%20learning%20strategy%20depends%20on%20learning%20goals%20and%20processes2.pdf

## C13 (vote 0-0)
The findings provide empirical support for the Knowledge-Learning-Instruction (KLI) framework (Koedinger et al., 2012), which holds that the relative efficacy of retrieval practice versus repeated studying depends on the kind of knowledge being learned — a theoretical basis for designing card types by knowledge category rather than uniformly.
출처: https://andymatuschak.org/files/papers/Yeo,%20Fazio%20-%202019%20-%20The%20optimal%20learning%20strategy%20depends%20on%20learning%20goals%20and%20processes2.pdf

## C14 (vote 0-0)
The study used complex, course-relevant educational material (research-methodology terminology and concepts) rather than simple word-pair stimuli, and benefits were measured over a multi-day delay — extending retrieval-practice transfer findings to authentic, complex conceptual learning. This addresses (partially) the far-transfer/원거리전이 concern, though the domain is research methods, not law, so transfer to legal 포섭 remains an inference, not a demonstrated finding.
출처: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475225001434

## C15 (vote 0-0)
Studying quiz questions together with their answers (quiz-study, analogous to passively reading Q+A cards) was inferior to actually attempting retrieval — restudy and quiz-study did not match the active-retrieval condition. This supports designing cards that force generation (e.g., production/cloze over passive read-through) in the card-wiki-pipeline.
출처: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475225001434

## C16 (vote 0-0)
Anki use is consistently associated with higher USMLE Step 1 (foundational/factual standardized exam) scores, with a dose-response effect — one study found each additional ~1,700 cards reviewed was associated with a one-point score increase, and high-frequency users outperformed minimal users by roughly 4-13 points across three studies.
출처: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40670-026-02643-5

## C17 (vote 0-0)
The worked-example effect is framed by Cognitive Load Theory as most advantageous for novices, because examples reduce cognitive load relative to problem solving and thereby facilitate knowledge retention.
출처: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01443410.2023.2273762

## C18 (vote 0-0)
A worked example was operationally defined as comprising three components — a problem statement, the solution steps, and the final solution — i.e., the full demonstrated solution path rather than the answer alone.
출처: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/01443410.2023.2273762

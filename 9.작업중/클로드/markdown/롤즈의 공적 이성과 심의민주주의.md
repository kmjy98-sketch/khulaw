## Rawls’s Public Reason and Deliberative Democracy 

## Myung-Sik Kim 

This paper looks at Rawls’s idea of public reason. One of the tasks for deliberative democracy is .to reach social consensus through citizens’ public reasoning. .The fact of pluralism makes social consensus difficult. Rawls attempted to achieve this task by introducing the principles of justice into public reasoning. He developed strategies of double exclusion: restricting the object of consensus to fundamental political issues and excluding comprehensive doctrines from public reasoning. His attempt to relate the principles of justice to the content of public reason, however, seems not satisfactory. Furthermore, public reasoning without comprehensive doctrines can not solve fundamental. political issues, which gives rise to the problem of completeness. Rawls. did not recognize the role of deliberation for the promotion of civility. We should acknowledge that mioral. disagreement is not an obstacle to avoid or overcome, but: a condition to live with. 

Key words : Public Reason, Rawls, Deliberative Democracy
